Relay is a personal video publishing studio for the creator behind TacosGuy. Prepare a video and caption, choose its audience, and send it to your connected channel.

Open the private studio ↗

The studio requires sign-in. This public information page does not connect accounts or accept credentials.

Why YouTube access?

Relay uses Google OAuth to identify the authorized channel, upload videos you submit, and read their processing and visibility status. It requests YouTube upload and read-only permissions.

You stay in control.

Posting begins when you submit a video in the private studio. You choose visibility and the made-for-kids setting. You can revoke Google access or remove saved credentials.

Built for personal use.

This integration is operated for TacosGuy’s own publishing workflow. It is not an open registration service. Google and YouTube can apply quotas, review requirements, and upload-visibility restrictions.

Read the privacy policy for details about the Google data Relay accesses, stores, and shares, and the terms of use for service limitations.
