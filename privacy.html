Privacy policy · Updated 10 November 2026

Your channel.

Relay is a personal video publishing tool operated by the creator of TacosGuy. This policy describes the current private studio and its Google/YouTube integration.

Information accessed

With your authorization, Relay accesses your YouTube channel identity and video processing and visibility information. It receives OAuth access and refresh tokens and the OAuth client credentials you save. The private studio also uses the identity supplied by its ChatGPT sign-in provider to keep each user’s stored records separate.

How information is used

Channel information verifies which account is connected. Access tokens authorize API requests. Refresh tokens renew access automatically while Google permits it. When you submit a post, Relay sends your chosen video, title, description, visibility, and audience setting to YouTube and checks the result. These permissions are not used to access Gmail, Drive, or unrelated Google services.

Storage and retention

Saved credentials and server-side job records are encrypted at rest in Cloudflare R2 and separated by the authenticated studio identity. Credentials remain until removed from Connections. Job metadata is retained to track upload status and prevent duplicate posts. Video bytes stream to the selected provider and are not saved in Relay’s R2 storage. Drafts and some preferences are stored in the browser.

Sharing and service providers

Google receives authorization requests and API requests, including the submitted video and its metadata. The studio’s hosting and storage infrastructure processes information needed to operate the service, and ChatGPT provides studio sign-in. When another platform is separately connected and selected, Relay sends the post to that platform as requested. Relay does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI models.

Relay’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

Use Remove in Relay’s Connections page to delete the saved platform credentials. You can also revoke authorization at Google Account connections. Revoking Google access does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube. Manage those videos in YouTube Studio. For deletion of other stored studio records or questions about this policy, contact the operator through the TacosGuy channel.

This public website

These information pages contain no account forms, analytics scripts, advertising, or credential storage. Their hosting provider may process visitor IP addresses and security logs under its own privacy policy. If hosted on GitHub Pages, see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the integration’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
